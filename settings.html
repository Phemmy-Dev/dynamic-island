Views

Scroll over the island to move between these, top to bottom. Now playing only appears while something is playing.

    Auto-hide

    The island tucks into a thin line over these apps so it doesn't cover their tabs. Rest the mouse on the line to open it.

    Tuck the island away
    One per line, as shown in Task Manager › Details, without ".exe".

    Now Playing

    Now Playing screen
    The full-screen clock, album art and lyrics. Opens after 2 minutes idle while music plays, from the island, or with Ctrl + Alt + M.
    Lyrics timing
    Slide right if lyrics appear late, left if early.

    Weather

    From Open-Meteo (free, no account). Only the city you type is sent. Leave it empty to turn weather off.
